## 📌 Project Overview

The **Global Retail Sales Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, discounts, shipping costs, and returned orders across different regions and markets.

The project focuses on transforming raw retail transaction data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive visualizations.

The dashboard helps management identify underperforming regions, evaluate discount-related losses, monitor returns, and make data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, profit, and quantity sold.
- Evaluate regional and customer segment performance.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Understand the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Monitor returned orders and regional return performance.
- Analyze shipping costs across different shipping modes.
- Support business decisions through interactive reporting.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Measures and calculated columns
- **Data Modeling** – Relationships between multiple tables
- **Data Visualization** – Charts, KPIs, and interactive reports

## 📂 Dataset & Data Model

The project uses three main tables:

1. **Orders Table:** Contains order transactions, order and shipping dates, customer information, product details, sales, quantity, discounts, profit, and shipping costs.
2. **Returns Table:** Contains return-related information used to analyze returned orders.
3. **People Table:** Contains regional manager information used for regional performance analysis.

Relationships between the tables enable combined analysis of sales, returns, and regional performance.

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Transformation

The following data preparation steps were performed:

- Handled missing values.
- Removed duplicate records.
- Standardized date and numeric data types.
- Cleaned and transformed relevant columns.
- Created relationships between tables.
- Developed calculated columns and DAX measures for analysis.

## 🧮 Key DAX Measures

| Measure | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | Calculates overall sales revenue |
| Total Profit | Calculates overall profit |
| Total Quantity | Calculates total units sold |
| Profit Margin % | Evaluates profitability relative to sales |
| Return Rate % | Measures the percentage of returned orders |
| Average Shipping Cost | Analyzes average shipping expenses |
| Sales Target | Establishes a benchmark for sales performance |

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

**1. Regional Performance**
- Central was the highest-profit region in the analysis.
- Canada was the lowest-profit region in the analysis.

**2. Product Performance**
- Apple Smart Phone was the top-selling product.
- Technology was the top-performing product category by sales.

**3. Customer Segments**
- Consumer was the most profitable customer segment.

**4. Shipping Performance**
- Standard Class was identified as the most cost-effective shipping mode.

**5. Returns Analysis**
- Central had the highest return performance, highlighting the need for further investigation into return causes.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- **Improve regional profitability:** Investigate Canada's performance and optimize pricing, product mix, and discount strategies.
- **Focus on high-performing products:** Use demand patterns to support inventory planning for Technology products and top-selling items.
- **Strengthen the Consumer segment:** Develop targeted strategies to retain customers and improve sales.
- **Optimize shipping costs:** Encourage Standard Class shipping when delivery urgency permits.
- **Investigate returns:** Analyze the reasons for returns in Central and identify opportunities to reduce them.
- **Control excessive discounts:** Evaluate discount policies to minimize profit losses and improve margins.

This project demonstrates how Power BI can transform retail transaction data into actionable insights. It provides a centralized view of sales, profitability, returns, and regional performance, helping stakeholders identify business challenges and make informed decisions.
